    Hubs

    Evanlite Straightpull Centerlock Ratchet 36T

    12x100, 12x142, Centerlock
    Support – which hub model to choose?

    All hub models use the axle standard 12×100 (front) and 12×142 (rear). Disc mounting: Centerlock.
    This is the most common specification used in modern road and gravel bikes. If your bike requires a different axle type or disc mount, you can select the correct end caps or adapters in the last step of the configurator – “Accessories”.

    Evanlite — Very lightweight hubs with a ratchet mechanism based on two independent discs, similar in design to the DT 350. High-quality TPI bearings with double sealing ensure durability and resistance to dirt.

    DT Swiss 350 — A well-known and popular hub with an excellent price-to-performance ratio. Available in two color options: black and black-white. The black version is produced by DT Swiss especially for Evanlite and features Evanlite branding. Ratchet system with two independent discs.

    DT Swiss 240 EXP — The top model in the DT Swiss range. Lighter than the 350 version, with the Ratchet EXP system, where one ratchet disc is integrated into the hub body.

    Ratchet — All hub models are equipped with a 36T ratchet, providing precise engagement and a large contact area for durability and reliability. Optionally, you can upgrade to a 54T ratchet in the configurator under “Accessories”.

    Bearings — All hub models use high-quality steel bearings with double labyrinth sealing (LLB). We do not use ceramic bearings. Regardless of brand, these are hybrid designs (ceramic balls with steel races) that tend to wear after around 3000 km, increase rolling resistance, and require replacement. In real use, the difference in friction is minimal, while steel bearings offer much higher durability.

    Spokes

    Sapim D-Light

    Straightpull
    Support – which spokes to choose?

    All spoke models used in Evanlite wheels are Straightpull spokes — for better aerodynamics, higher stiffness, and increased durability.

    Sapim D-Light — Spokes from a well-known Belgian manufacturer. A very good balance of low weight and strength. Double-butted spokes: 2.0×1.65×2.0 mm.

    DT Aerocomp — High stiffness and maximum strength. A good choice for race wheels where stiffness under high power matters, and also for heavier riders who need increased spoke durability. Heavier than Sapim D-Light by 30 grams (per wheelset). Bladed profile.

    DT Aerolite — The top spoke model from DT Swiss. Very lightweight. Recommended when the lowest possible wheel weight is the priority, while still keeping good durability. Lighter than Sapim D-Light by 30 grams (per wheelset). Bladed profile.

    FAQ

    Will the wheels fit my bike?

    Yes! Our road and gravel wheels use the most common hub axle standard: 12×100 mm at the front and 12×142 mm at the rear. For MTB wheels, we use the Boost axle standard. For road wheels with rim brakes (V-brake), the current standard is QR.

    If you need a different axle size, you can choose the correct adapter (end caps) in the configurator. We will adjust the hubs to match your bike’s standard.
    How long does production and shipping take after placing an order?

    During configuration and ordering, the estimated lead time is shown — usually just a few days. Each wheelset is built individually.

    Delivery by courier usually takes around 2–3 business days within the European Union.
    Are carbon rims durable?

    Carbon is a material that combines two key benefits: high strength and low weight. That is why it has been the base of modern racing components for many years. To damage a well-designed carbon rim, a very strong impact is needed.

    Before any Evanlite wheelset goes to market, it passes a series of tests that go beyond industry standards. We test rim impacts without a tire, extreme load simulations, maximum tire pressure, and rim wall expansion. On top of that, we perform many real-world test rides on different terrain — even beyond the intended use of the model. Our goal is maximum strength and long-term durability.
    What is the Warranty?

    Good wheels are an investment for many years, and we offer one of the most effective carbon wheel warranties on the market. After purchase, your wheels are automatically covered by the Evanlite Protector program.

    If damage occurs while riding within 2 years from purchase — whether during a race crash or a training fall — we will repair the wheels free of charge. The warranty also covers possible material defects that may appear later over time.

    More details can be found on the page dedicated to the warranty program.
